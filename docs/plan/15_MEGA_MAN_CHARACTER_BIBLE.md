# Mega Man character bible

Status: **future kit and behavior baseline v1, 2026-10-13; production deferred**

This is a retained future roster packet. Production is deferred until the
Mushroom Kingdom and Dream Land opening/breadth shelves in plan `16` are
complete. *Mega Man 2* remains selected because its
native sprite vocabulary is unusually compatible with the desktop strip and
its eight Robot Masters expand reusable projectile, shield, charge and control
systems. All ultimate values use the corrected rarity floors in plan `02`.

Numerical values are **TRIAL**. Listed HP and Attack are Training Rank 1,
zero-star values after rarity. Ultimate numbers are Technique Mastery 1; rank 5
adds 25% potency or two seconds to a transformation under the common rules.

## 2. Special Weapon rule

The eight boss weapons are primarily Power-Ups, not eight shallow Mega Man
fighter forms. A compatible Mega Man-family fighter equips one at a time; it
replaces or augments the skill and changes the correct palette/projectile.

| Weapon | Power-Up effect direction | MM2 boss interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bubble Lead | ground-following shot; reveals low/hidden targets | Heat Man weakness |
| Air Shooter | rising spread; bonus stagger to Airborne | Crash Man weakness |
| Quick Boomerang | fast returning basics for a timed window | Metal Man weakness |
| Crash Bomber | persistent planted explosive; Guard Break | Flash Man/Wily utility |
| Time Stopper | one bounded enemy freeze/major Slow | Quick Man weakness |
| Metal Blade | eight-direction piercing/returning blade | Bubble/Flash/Wood utility |
| Leaf Shield | four-hit shield that can be launched | Air Man weakness |
| Atomic Fire | charged piercing fire shot | Wood Man weakness |

Weaknesses are authored title-local facts, not a universal element triangle.
The matching weapon gives **+35% damage and +30% stagger** to that boss. Every
story fight remains clearable with the Buster/free roster; the weakness rewards
collection and observation instead of acting as a key.

Special Weapons use cooldowns and visible weapon energy but no permanent ammo
consumable. Their energy refills between waves and through authored capsules,
so an auto-battle cannot waste a limited account resource.

## 4. Mega Man 2 fighters

### Mega Man — Blue Bomber · R

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `mega-man`. **Role:** Striker / Shot,
either row. **Stats:** HP 840; ATK 116; Guard 30; Move 128; Haste 4%; Crit 10%.

- **Basic — Mega Buster:** 0.92× Shot at 420 px; can destroy declared weak
  projectiles and fires only after the arm-cannon pose locks.
- **Skill — Charged Buster, 10 s:** plants his feet and fires a 2.20× piercing
  shot with 18 stagger. An equipped Special Weapon replaces this record.
- **Passive — Weapon Ready:** matching a declared weakness adds the common 35%
  damage/30% stagger. Weapon energy refills 25% at wave start.
- **Ultimate — Buster Overdrive:** four 0.80× running shots, a slide-through
  reposition and one 2.00× full charge. Focused total: **5.20×**.
- **Power-Ups:** all eight MM2 Special Weapons; later adapter/armor tags.
- **Reveal/feel:** teleport beam resolves into idle before the first shot;
  quote “Systems ready.” Running and firing can overlap, damage begins at the
  muzzle flash, and recoil/palette always match the equipped weapon.

### Sniper Joe — Shield-Line Sentry · R

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `sniper-joe`. **Role:** Vanguard / Shot,
front. **Stats:** HP 1,180; ATK 82; Guard 68; Move 100; Haste 0%; Crit 6%.

- **Basic — Guarded Shot:** lowers shield, fires a 0.95× round, then raises it.
- **Skill — Shield Counter, 10 s:** guards for 1.2 s; an intercepted frontal hit
  triggers a 1.80× answer. No-hit fallback advances and fires for 0.75×.
- **Passive — Sentry Shield:** frontal damage is reduced 45% while not firing;
  attacks from behind or during the exposed muzzle window ignore it.
- **Ultimate — Formation Advance:** two Joe echoes join a four-volley march at
  1.00× each, then all three brace to reduce party frontal projectile damage
  35% for 5 s. Damage total: **4.00×**, plus protection.
- **Reveal/feel:** the shield flash precedes the eye slit; quote “Target
  acquired.” Shield-up, exposed and counter frames are distinct actor states.

### Bubble Man — Depth-Pressure Hunter · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `bubble-man`. **Role:** Controller / Shot,
either. **Stats:** HP 978; ATK 115; Guard 30; Move 118; Haste 3%; Crit 9%.

- **Basic — Bubble Bounce:** 0.85× Shot bounces once along the ground for an
  optional 0.35× second hit and passes beneath high projectile cover.
- **Skill — Bubble Lead, 9 s:** sends three ground-following bubbles at 0.65×;
  each reveals hidden/vanished targets and applies 15% Slow for 3 s.
- **Passive — Pressure Suit:** gains 15 Guard and 12% Move in Aquatic stages;
  outside water, landing after a jump releases one harmless visual bubble.
- **Ultimate — Pressure Cascade:** six 0.90× bubbles converge, then burst for
  1.20× and 4 s Slow. Focused total: **6.60×**.
- **Reveal/feel:** water line sweeps over the card; quote “Pressure rising.”
  Bubbles deform on bounce and never travel like recolored bullets.

### Air Man — Storm-Fan Fortress · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `air-man`. **Role:** Artillery / Lob,
back. **Stats:** HP 874; ATK 132; Guard 24; Move 110; Haste 1%; Crit 9%.

- **Basic — Air Shooter:** launches two rising tornadoes for 0.52× each.
- **Skill — Fan Wall, 11 s:** spins the torso fan for 2.5 s, pushing Small
  enemies and destroying/deflecting up to three weak projectiles; ends with a
  1.10× gust.
- **Passive — No Easy Approach:** enemies displaced by Air Man take 20% more
  stagger from the next Charge or collision within 4 s.
- **Ultimate — Typhoon Array:** eight 0.75× tornadoes fill alternating heights,
  then a 1.00× fan burst forces survivors outward. Focused total: **7.00×**.
- **Reveal/feel:** fan silhouette spins up before cloud bands separate; quote
  “Try to get through this.” Tornadoes have lift and curved paths.

### Quick Man — Time-Slice Pursuer · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `quick-man`. **Role:** Striker / Charge,
front. **Stats:** HP 943; ATK 140; Guard 24; Move 160; Haste 8%; Crit 15%.

- **Basic — Quick Boomerang:** 0.80× outbound and 0.40× return; Quick Man can
  begin moving before the catch but loses it if interrupted.
- **Skill — Time-Slice Dash, 8 s:** wall-kicks across the line for 2.20× and
  gains 20% dodge only during travel.
- **Passive — Never Still:** uninterrupted actions build three Speed marks;
  each grants 4% damage. Stun or a Heavy collision removes all marks.
- **Ultimate — Quick Time Assault:** seven 0.90× dashes/boomerang contacts and
  a 1.80× crossing finisher. Focused total: **8.10×**.
- **Reveal/feel:** red boomerang cuts the black screen before he appears; quote
  “Too slow.” Motion uses wall contact, braking and afterimages, not teleporting
  hitboxes.

### Crash Man — Delayed-Impact Demolition · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `crash-man`. **Role:** Artillery / Lob,
back. **Stats:** HP 897; ATK 130; Guard 30; Move 115; Haste 2%; Crit 10%.

- **Basic — Crash Bomber:** sticks for 0.80×, flashes twice, then bursts for
  0.55× splash. Only three of his bombs may persist simultaneously.
- **Skill — Structural Failure, 11 s:** plants bombs on two lane anchors; after
  2 s each deals 1.20× and Guard Break for 5 s. Early target death transfers a
  bomb to the nearest destructible object rather than wasting it.
- **Passive — Chain Reaction:** an explosion hitting another planted bomb adds
  20% radius and 15 stagger, once per skill cycle.
- **Ultimate — Crash Chain:** six 0.85× bombs connect target-to-target and
  detonate into a 2.40× center blast. Focused total: **7.50×**, plus Guard
  Break.
- **Reveal/feel:** warning lights count down audibly; quote “Stand clear.” Bombs
  remain visible objects and never become unexplained delayed damage.

### Flash Man — Stolen-Second Controller · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `flash-man`. **Role:** Controller / Shot,
either. **Stats:** HP 1,001; ATK 115; Guard 36; Move 106; Haste 2%; Crit 8%.

- **Basic — Crystal Buster:** 0.90× Shot; gains 15% Crit against Slowed targets.
- **Skill — Flash Window, 12 s:** pauses Small enemies for 1.5 s. Elites are
  Slowed 35%; bosses receive 25 stagger and -20% Haste for 3 s.
- **Passive — Borrowed Time:** each enemy affected by control grants 3 energy,
  capped at 12 per skill use.
- **Ultimate — Time Stopper:** Small enemies freeze for 4 s; elites/bosses lose
  50% Haste/Move and gain 60 stagger. During the window Flash Man fires six
  0.60× shots and the party gains 30% Haste. Damage total: **3.60×**; control
  and free allied actions carry it above the SR value floor.
- **Reveal/feel:** clock ticks vanish, particles stop, then color drains; quote
  “This second is mine.” The game simulation slows/freezes declared actors—the
  effect is not merely a blue overlay.

### Metal Man — Eight-Way Executioner · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `metal-man`. **Role:** Striker / Shot,
either. **Stats:** HP 897; ATK 138; Guard 26; Move 130; Haste 5%; Crit 14%.

- **Basic — Metal Return:** blade deals 0.90× outbound and 0.45× returning.
- **Skill — Angle Read, 9 s:** throws three blades along chosen low/mid/high
  paths for 0.75× each; AI selects paths containing distinct targets first.
- **Passive — Surgical Edge:** returning hits against a previously struck
  target gain 20% Crit and 15% projectile speed.
- **Ultimate — Metal Cyclone:** eight blades converge for 0.85× each, then the
  caught center blade is thrown again for 1.50×. Focused total: **8.30×**.
- **Reveal/feel:** blade ring closes around the portrait; quote “One angle is
  enough.” Every blade travels and can be intercepted independently.

### Wood Man — Forest-Core Bulwark · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `wood-man`. **Role:** Vanguard / Lob,
front. **Stats:** HP 1,380; ATK 106; Guard 70; Move 92; Haste -1%; Crit 6%.

- **Basic — Leaf Fall:** 0.90× Lob plus 0.40× splash from above.
- **Skill — Leaf Shield, 11 s:** creates five leaves, each blocking one hit or
  dealing 0.45× contact damage. Remaining leaves launch after 4 s.
- **Passive — Rooted Frame:** gains 20 Guard while stationary and cannot be
  displaced by ordinary wind; Fire removes the bonus for 3 s.
- **Ultimate — Forest Crash:** five 0.90× giant leaves descend, then Wood Man
  body-slams for 2.00× and grants the party a 70% ATK shield. Focused total:
  **6.50×** damage; the four-person shield adds 2.80× conservative support
  value for **9.30× total combat value**.
- **Reveal/feel:** leaf ring forms one piece at a time; quote “Hold your ground.”
  Shield leaves are persistent, countable actors rather than a green aura.

### Heat Man — Atomic-Flame Charger · SR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `heat-man`. **Role:** Bruiser / Charge,
front. **Stats:** HP 1,208; ATK 127; Guard 44; Move 120; Haste 2%; Crit 9%.

- **Basic — Furnace Breath:** four 0.25× Beam ticks and Burn.
- **Skill — Ignition Charge, 10 s:** closes the lid for 0.6 s, then travels
  through the nearest line for 2.10×. He resists control during travel, not
  during ignition.
- **Passive — White Hot:** damaging Burning enemies adds one Heat stack, up to
  three; each adds 5% skill damage. A wave transition clears them.
- **Ultimate — Atomic Furnace:** for 10 s gains 50% ATK, 30% Haste and Burn
  immunity; basics become 1.30× flame charges. The form ends with a 2.50×
  eruption and targets **7–10 normal-action equivalents**.
- **Reveal/feel:** darkness shows only lid seams before ignition; quote “Now
  we're cooking.” Open, closed and charging silhouettes remain distinct.

### Dr. Wily — Machine No. 2 · SSR

**Source:** *Mega Man 2*. **Family:** `wily`. **Role:** Artillery / Summon,
back. **Stats:** HP 1,449; ATK 152; Guard 50; Move 88; Haste 0%; Crit 8%.

- **Basic — Machine Barrage:** three 0.45× cannon shots at separate heights.
- **Skill — Gear Press, 12 s:** deploys two destructible gears that roll toward
  priority targets for 1.10× each and briefly body-block Small enemies.
- **Passive — Contingency Capsule:** once per encounter below 30% HP, ejects for
  5 s, losing 25 Guard but gaining 30% dodge and backline distance. He returns
  to Machine No. 2 afterward; it is not a revive.
- **Ultimate — Alien Projection:** blacks out the arena, projects the alien and
  fires eight 0.85× energy orbs plus a 2.70× machine laser. Focused total:
  **9.50×**.
- **Reveal/feel:** eyebrow silhouette, machine lights, false alien outline;
  quote “You have seen nothing yet.” Playable scale is declared separately from
  the multi-phase boss record.

## 5. 20XX Archive additions

### Roll — Workshop First Responder · R

**Source:** *Mega Man Powered Up*. **Family:** `roll`. **Role:** Support / Combo,
either. **Stats:** HP 900; ATK 78; Guard 34; Move 122; Haste 5%; Crit 7%.

- **Basic — Broom Combo:** three 0.40× sweeps, the last lightly pushing Small
  enemies away from the weakest ally.
- **Skill — Emergency Maintenance, 10 s:** repairs the lowest-HP Mechanical ally
  for 100% ATK; non-Mechanical allies receive an 80% ATK heal. Cleanses Burn.
- **Passive — Spare Parts:** the first destroyed projectile/summon each wave
  drops a part that gives the lowest-HP ally a 35% ATK shield.
- **Ultimate — Workshop Rescue:** heals the party for 200% ATK, shields for
  120% ATK, cleanses one condition and advances all allied skill cooldowns by
  3 s. Its support value clears the R ultimate floor.
- **Reveal/feel:** workshop shutters open into the card; quote “Let me fix
  that.” Repair tools and broom contacts exist physically in her animations.

### Proto Man — Whistling Wanderer · SSR

**Source:** *Mega Man 3*. **Family:** `proto-man`. **Role:** Striker / Shot,
either. **Stats:** HP 1,242; ATK 159; Guard 32; Move 138; Haste 6%; Crit 13%.

- **Basic — Proto Buster:** 1.00× Shot; every third basic is a 1.35× charged
  shot with a longer exposed recovery.
- **Skill — Shield Breakthrough, 10 s:** blocks frontal projectiles while
  charging, then deals 2.00× through the first target. Rear hits still connect.
- **Passive — Lone Signal:** begins each wave with 15 energy. When no other
  Heroic-tag ally is present, gains 10% Crit rather than a raw rarity bonus.
- **Ultimate — Blues Breakthrough:** whistle cue, five 1.10× charged shots and
  a 3.00× shield dash. Focused total: **8.50×**.
- **Reveal/feel:** the whistle plays over black before scarf and shield enter;
  quote “Keep moving.” Shield orientation, scarf direction and charged muzzle
  frames must agree with movement.

## 7. Production and acceptance order

1. destructible/interceptable projectile primitive;
2. Buster fire while running, charged windup and palette/projectile swap;
3. returning blade/boomerang path and catch;
4. planted bomb actor with bounded chain reaction;
5. shield orientation and exposed windows;
6. title-local weakness and Special Weapon replacement;
7. bounded time-stop simulation state;
8. Robot Master entrance/defeat/Weapon Get presentation;
9. multi-zone fortress bosses;
10. Wily playable/boss scale separation.

Before the roster is approved:

- Mega Man can run and shoot without sliding or spawning shots from his torso;
- all eight weapon Power-Ups swap palette, projectile and Manual text;
- weakness raises damage/stagger but every boss clears with the free Buster
  fixture at expected Training Rank;
- Quick Man remains on-screen at maximum Move/Haste and visibly touches walls;
- Flash Man freezes only eligible states and every actor resumes correctly;
- Crash bombs and Wood leaves survive save-independent frame drops without
  duplicating or remaining forever;
- Wily's ultimate reaches at least seven ordinary basic-action equivalents in
  single-target simulation;
- Roll and Proto Man cards identify their non-MM2 source and never appear as
  enemies in the MM2 World.
