# Zelda Saga planner

Status: **candidate museum and World spine v1, 2026-10-14; not in production**

Zelda can support a large Saga because each game supplies locations, items,
companions, enemies and distinct Link/Zelda/Ganon interpretations. It should
not become sixty Links distinguished only by tunic color. Masks, dungeon tools
and passive gear stay Power-Ups unless they change the entire actor grammar.

The first production World is *A Link to the Past*. No Zelda banner opens until
Mushroom and Dream Land release gates are met.

## 1. Scale

- **Core Story spine:** 300 stages across fourteen source Worlds.
- **Curated fighter museum:** 67 cards, roughly 4.48 Story stages per card.
- **Normal Supply direction:** 24 Power-Ups and 18 Assist Pages.
- **First complete World:** *A Link to the Past*, 25 stages, 9–13 new fighters,
  10–16 Supply records and five boss packets.

The ratio is intentionally near the upper healthy boundary. Zelda's combat
variety comes from dungeon rules, items and enemy behaviors as much as from
pullable characters. Add fighters only when their roaming animation and kit are
credible.

## 10. First production packet: A Link to the Past

Do not fully stat the 67-card museum first. The initial 25-stage packet needs:

- fighters: ALTTP Link, Zelda, Agahnim, Ganon plus selected Stalfos/Darknut/
  Wizzrobe or named boss recruits only after their enemy actors prove depth;
- Power-Ups: Heart Container, Bottled Fairy, Pegasus Boots, Mirror Shield,
  Fire Rod and Ice Rod;
- Assists: Sahasrahla, Great Fairy and one dungeon-guide Page;
- engine proofs: directional shield, reflected projectile ownership, charged
  spin, tether/boomerang secondary actor, room hazard zones and light/dark
  palette transition;
- assets: SNES-native fighters/enemies first; no Wild-era custom sprite work in
  the same milestone.

The exact opening Archive count and numerical cards are authored only when
Zelda reaches production Wave. Until then, every row above remains `candidate`.
