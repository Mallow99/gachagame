SSR King Dedede Self-proclaimed King of Dream Land

The rig

One flat official render, no layers. It sits on a 46×52 mesh trimmed to his silhouette, with eight warp deformers driving it: breathing, body sway, head tilt, hammer wind-up, fist pump, robe-hem flutter, a squash on the breath, and a blink drawn over the eyes.

The splash

Built after the Todo SSR loop: the character barely moves, and the life comes from layers around him. Streaks, drifting stars, sparkles, embers, foreground silhouettes, rim light, smoke and a slow camera drift, all on a graded Castle Dedede arena.

Sources

Render from Kirby's Return to Dream Land Deluxe. Arena from Kirby Star Allies, blurred and recolored. Both via WiKirby.
